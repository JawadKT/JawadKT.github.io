something i care about

Growing up, I didn't really know what mental health was. Nobody around me talked about it, and that's pretty common in South Asian families.

In 2023 I started South Asian Saffron Minds, a youth-led nonprofit focused on mental health for South Asian youth. Since then we've run workshops at community centres and schools, written blogs, raised funding through pitches and grants, and built a team that's now spread across the world.
