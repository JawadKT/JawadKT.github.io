the thing i care about most

Growing up, I didn't really know what mental health was. It just never got talked about, and in a lot of South Asian families it still doesn't. So I started South Asian Saffron Minds to change that for young people.

It grew into workshops at community centres and schools, a bunch of blog posts, funding from pitches and grants, and a team spread across the world.
